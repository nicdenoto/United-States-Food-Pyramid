# WG-01 Stage 3 Stress Test — EvidenceRecord Retrofit (schema rev 3)

**What this is:** the actual retrofit of WG-01's existing corpus record
(`corpus/claim-inventory.md`) into `EvidenceRecord`s under
`src/schema/evidence-record.md` (rev 3). This is not a redesign exercise —
it is the test of whether rev 3's decisions hold up against real numbers,
not just the three curated examples the schema doc itself uses. 28 records
below, chosen to hit the actual edge cases WG-01 contains: pooled
meta-analyses, a meta-analysis *of* RCTs, an exposure-mismatch case
(fibre vs. whole grain specifically), derivation chains, competing/
alternative estimates, qualitative mechanistic/limitation/caveat records,
and per-study nested data. Findings — where the schema held up cleanly and
where it didn't — are in §2.

This file does not yet replace `corpus/claim-inventory.md` as the
authoritative record; it sits alongside it as the retrofit output for
review. `claim-inventory.md` stays authoritative until this is approved.

---

### 1.5 Reynolds' RCT arm — whole-grain-specific vs. total-dietary-fibre (the exposure-mismatch case)

**CORRECTED during the final audit pass (2026-09-15); page numbers below
further corrected during the full reconciliation pass (see §5).** WG01-EV-018
and WG01-EV-019 originally used numbers that turned out to be Reynolds'
**glycaemic-index RCT panel** (Table 3, same page as Table 2 -- both p.438;
Figure 4C is the companion forest plot, p.442) — not the whole-grain panel
(Table 2, p.438; Figure 4B, p.442) — this error was already present in
`claim-inventory.md` before this stress test and was carried into the first
retrofit pass without independent re-verification. Caught by directly
re-tracing every record to its exact source location per the final-pass
checklist (criterion 1) rather than trusting the prior citation. See
`claim-inventory.md`'s corresponding correction for the full account.
Corrected below; `claim-inventory.md` fixed separately.

## 2. Findings

**What held up cleanly:**

1. **Meta-analysis-of-RCTs (WG01-EV-018/019).** `study_design: RCT` +
   `synthesis_design: meta_analysis` is a real, common combination
   (Reynolds' own whole-grain RCT tables are exactly this), and the split
   handles it without friction — this is precisely the case rev 3's split
   was designed for, and it worked on the first real example, not just the
   cohort case it was originally motivated by.
2. **Derivation chains (WG01-EV-007→008, WG01-EV-010→005).** Filing the
   Reynolds per-15g figure as its own `SOURCE_REPORTED` record and the
   DGA's per-30g harmonization as a separate `SOURCE_DERIVED` record
   pointing back at it worked cleanly for both CRC and T2D, not just the
   one case the schema doc originally used as its worked example.
3. **`recommendation_citation`'s three-way split earned its keep.** Beyond
   the `CITED_DIRECTLY` / `NOT_CITED` split GPT originally proposed,
   `CITED_INDIRECTLY` turned out to have a real, recurring use: every
   record that *feeds* a derivation (WG01-EV-007, 010) or a pooled figure
   (WG01-EV-025, 026) but isn't itself the tabulated number is
   `CITED_INDIRECTLY` — a distinct, common pattern, not a rare edge case.
4. **Qualitative records (WG01-EV-009, 022, 023, 024) all filed cleanly**
   under `extraction_status: SOURCE_REPORTED` with no awkwardness — this
   directly validates dropping `NOT_APPLICABLE` in rev 3. WG01-EV-023 in
   particular turns what was previously free-floating prose (the
   "unresolved evidentiary question" flagged in the last `claim-inventory`
   pass) into an actual structured, queryable record — a concrete win, not
   just a formality.
5. **Exposure-mismatch evidence (WG01-EV-020/021 vs. 018/019) needed no
   new fields.** The existing `exposure` field on `study_classification`
   was enough to represent "same outcome, different but related exposure"
   cleanly, and `recommendation_citation: NOT_CITED` correctly flags that
   the DGA's own evidence chain doesn't rely on this fibre-general
   evidence — even though it's directly relevant to WG-01a's mechanistic-
   specificity question.

**Real friction, worth a decision before this is frozen:**

1. **Funding/COI is a source-level fact, filed on a per-record field.**
   `quality_signals[]` lives on the `EvidenceRecord`, but "Hu et al. report
   no COI and public funding only" is true of the whole paper, not of any
   one outcome estimate. Attaching it to WG01-EV-001 only (rather than
   also 003, 004, 006 — all from the same source) makes it look like a
   fact about mortality specifically. I left it off all four Hu records
   above rather than guess at the right answer. Recommend: this belongs on
   the *Source* record (Stage 2, `corpus/01_sources/`) as a source-level
   signal, not replicated across every `EvidenceRecord` drawn from that
   source. Needs a small addition to whatever the Source-record shape
   ends up being — out of scope for this schema doc, but worth flagging
   now rather than after the fact.
2. **Two-arm field naming assumes an interventional frame.** WG01-EV-025/
   026 use `raw_counts_two_arm` for single (non-pooled) cohort studies —
   `sample_size_intervention`/`comparator` read naturally for an RCT's
   randomized arms, less naturally for "highest quartile of exposure" vs.
   "lowest quartile" in an observational cohort. It's usable (I used it
   above), but the field names carry RCT connotations. Minor — not worth
   a redesign, but worth documenting explicitly in the schema that
   "intervention/comparator" means "higher-exposure/lower-exposure group"
   in a non-randomized single-study record, so a future extractor doesn't
   read it as RCT-only.
3. **`NOT_REPORTED`-across-multiple-sources is verbose by design.** The
   serving-to-gram gap is confirmed absent in five separate sources; filing
   one `NOT_REPORTED` record per source (WG01-EV-027, 028 here, for only 2
   of the 5) is the most rigorous option but means a genuinely simple fact
   ("nobody reports this") takes 5 near-identical records to state fully.
   I didn't build all 5 rather than guess whether that's the right
   trade-off. Live question for you and GPT: accept the verbosity (most
   rigorous, one `source_location` per check), or add a
   `checked_but_absent_in: [source_id, ...]` list variant for a single
   `NOT_REPORTED` record checked against multiple sources at once.
4. **New this pass: a pooled RCT meta-analysis can legitimately need BOTH
   `raw_counts_pooled`-shape and `raw_counts_two_arm`-shape facts at once,
   which rev 3's validation rule 10 ("exactly one shape present per
   record") doesn't allow.** WG01-EV-018/019/020/021 are each a
   meta-analysis of RCTs (`synthesis_design: meta_analysis`), so by rule
   10 they should carry `raw_counts_pooled`. But Reynolds' RCT tables
   report arm-level intervention/control participant totals directly
   (e.g. 498/421 for WG01-EV-018) rather than a single pooled N -- that's
   exactly what `raw_counts_two_arm` is for. Using `raw_counts_pooled`
   here would force `pooled_n_participants` to `NOT_REPORTED` even though
   the paper reports something very close to it (just split by arm, not
   summed), which felt like manufacturing an absence that isn't real; I
   used `raw_counts_two_arm` in all four records instead, which is more
   faithful to what the source actually tabulates. This is genuinely
   unresolved, not something I should decide unilaterally: it's a live
   design question for you and GPT, not a bug in my retrofit -- possible
   options include relaxing rule 10 to "one or both may be present,
   populated only from what the source reports" (simplest; matches what I
   actually did above), or adding a third combined shape for meta-
   analyses of RCTs specifically. I'd lean toward the first as the smaller
   change, but flagging rather than deciding.

**A corpus-completeness gap, not a schema-design flaw:**

5. **WG-01b (reduce refined carbohydrates) and WG-01d (chronic-disease
   carve-out) have zero `EvidenceRecord`s.** Every record in this stress
   test — and everything in `claim-inventory.md` before it — is `claim_id:
   WG-01a` (or, for the two serving-to-gram records, `WG-01c`). The
   four-part bundle framing has existed since early in this project, but
   in practice all extraction work so far has been on the whole-grain-
   prioritization claim and its five disease outcomes. This isn't
   something the stress test can fix — it's a scope gap the stress test
   makes visible by forcing every record to declare a `claim_id`, which
   `claim-inventory.md`'s prose format didn't force as clearly.

## 3. Final audit pass (2026-09-15) — against GPT's four-point checklist

Before recommending PASS, I re-traced every populated number and every
`NOT_REPORTED` in all 28 records directly against the primary-source PDFs
(via `pdftotext -layout`), rather than trusting the first retrofit pass.
Against GPT's four criteria:

1. **Every populated number traces to an exact source location.** Re-
   checked. This is what surfaced the Reynolds correction below — the
   original WG01-EV-018/019 numbers traced to a real location, just the
   wrong one (Table 3, not Table 2). Now corrected and re-verified; every
   number in this file traces to the `source_location` given.
2. **Every `NOT_REPORTED` is genuinely checked absence.** Re-checked
   against the actual PDFs, not the first pass's assumptions. Two
   corrections came out of this: Hu 2023's pooled study/participant/event
   counts (WG01-EV-001, 003, 004, 006) were available in the paper and
   had been blanket-marked `NOT_REPORTED` without a fresh look; and
   Schlesinger's per-outcome contributing-study counts (WG01-EV-014
   through 017) were missing from the retrofit entirely, with an
   unstated assumption that all 6 Table 5 studies fed every pooled
   estimate, which was wrong (5 feed obesity, 3 feed weight gain). Both
   fixed.
3. **No record contains a pipeline-performed calculation or statistical
   judgment.** Checked WG01-EV-005/008 (the DGA's 24%/6% derivations)
   specifically, per GPT's second scrutiny point. `derivation_description`
   now states plainly that the 24%/6% figures are the DGA's own disclosed
   arithmetic, that this pipeline verified that arithmetic against
   Reynolds' reported RR, and that the pipeline did not originate a
   calculation of its own.
4. **No field is used in a way that changes its meaning from the schema.**
   Checked the Schlesinger `raw_counts_two_arm` naming issue, per GPT's
   first scrutiny point. Added an explicit interpretation note in §1.7 and
   cross-referenced from §2 finding 2: `intervention`/`comparator` on
   those records means higher-exposure/lower-exposure cohort groups, not
   randomized arms, and `sample_size_total`/`events_total` are the only
   populated sub-fields for genuinely single-study observational records.

**The major finding from this pass: a real data error, not a schema
issue.** WG01-EV-018/019 (and `claim-inventory.md`'s underlying entry)
had mislabeled Reynolds' glycaemic-index RCT panel (Table 3/Figure 4C) as
whole-grain-specific RCT data (Table 2/Figure 4B) — confirmed by exact
numeric matching across three endpoints. Corrected, the true whole-grain
body-weight result is MD −0.62 (95% CI −1.19 to −0.05) — significant,
which reverses the prior "essentially null effects on every risk-factor
endpoint" reading of that evidence. This was inherited from before this
stress test and would not have surfaced without the source-by-source
re-trace criterion 1 required. `claim-inventory.md` is corrected to match.

**Schema verdict:** the schema itself held up well — every structural
decision from rev 1 through rev 3 survived contact with 28 real records
covering pooled meta-analyses, a meta-analysis of RCTs, derivation chains,
